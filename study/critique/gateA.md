**1. Scores (1-10):** hook 5 | clarity 4 | feel/juice 4 | visuals vs reference 3 | HUD/CTA/endcard 5 (light) | tech 8 | **overall 4**
**Why it reads as a prototype:** a pale-grey map with ants on it. (a) The squad is 3-4x too small: 1 m soldier = 89 px at start, 70-77 px in combat (3.0-3.8 % of H); reference soldiers are 14-17 % of H (ref3 ~105/619), lone ref4 ~7 %. (b) The squad is one navy-blue blob: helmet, jacket, arms, "face" all blue (back-of-head navy ~#2E5FB8-#4E68A0), no tan vest/skin/belts like ref; guns are dark sticks pointing away from the camera, so MINIGUN changes nothing visible on the squad. (c) Red crowd = ~12 visible 0.7 m figures, 2 % of H each (ref: 100+ at ~9 %), bowling-pin legs; "wave" never looks like a horde. (d) Boss reads as a pale ghost: dome pixel (232,205,196) s=0.16 vs recolour target #C73A30 s=0.75, beige legs, grey band, hidden blond spikes, buried under yellow ring spaghetti. (e) FX: 60-92 separate yellow dashes on screen in every combat frame (ref ~6-10), 47-58 hits/0.22 s at the boss, a full-width pale-yellow stomp band. (f) Empty world: no horizon/sky/city/cables in any frame (pitch+FOV: top ray still 31 deg below horizon), fog 34-100 m never touches a visible pixel, towers = flat maroon slabs, motion cue = 6 m dashes at 3 m/s (~230 px/s, one dash per ~2 s) and 4 towers in 17 s.
